The Knight — Hollow Knight Character for Slay the Spire 2
Current version: v0.6.6

Play as the silent vessel from Hallownest. Strike enemies with your Nail to gather SOUL, then spend it on spells, healing and companions.

New in v0.6.6
- SOUL costs use the original Hollow Knight vessel icon and centered, outlined numbers matching the game's Energy font.
- Fixes background SOUL badges appearing over enlarged cards. Discounted SOUL costs also match card descriptions.
- Moves the combat SOUL counter beside Energy and restores the Knight's health-bar width.
- Improves the grounded Knight silhouette and fast Nail slash animation; updates Deep Focus artwork and Nail Art names.
- Includes card balance and resource interaction fixes: repeatable-loop limits, a 24-HP companion cap, correct SOUL payment/replays and Nail combo/charge bonuses.
- Retains the stable-release Old Nail/card-play hang fix. Supports stable v0.107.1 and public-beta v0.111.0.

Content
- 104 card types: 101 reward-pool cards, 2 basic cards and the Weaver's Bite token.
- 25 exclusive relics, including the starter Soul Catcher.
- English and Simplified Chinese, following the game's language setting.
- Requires BaseLib v3.4.7 or newer. Please subscribe to the required BaseLib item.

The SOUL loop
- Nail hits gather 11 SOUL with the starter relic; the base SOUL cap is 99.
- Most spells cost 33 SOUL in addition to Energy. Spells and companion attacks do not gather Nail SOUL.
- Build around Nail combos, spell bursts, SOUL banking, Poison, shell defense, injury rewards or companions.

Companion summoning
- Weaverling, Grimmchild and Glowing Womb Hatchling each have independent HP bars.
- Weaverling attacks at turn end and supports multi-hit commands.
- Grimmchild matures through repeated summons: its flame deals 5, then 8, then 11 damage, with original stage artwork.
- Hatchlings guard first and explode on death or sacrifice.
- One living companion of each species. Repeated summoning heals and increases maximum HP up to 24.
- Companions intercept enemy attacks after your Block; overflow damage still hits you.
- Grow companions for health-based attacks, chain commands, sacrifice them for Block or resources, revive fallen companions and feed them SOUL.
- Companion attacks do not inherit Nail strength or extra Nail hits.

虫群召唤
织者幼虫、格林之子和孵化小虫各有独立血条。同种最多存活一只，重复召唤治疗并增加最大生命，最高24。格林之子成熟后火焰伤害由5提升到8、11并切换原作立绘。孵化小虫优先挡伤，死亡或牺牲时爆炸。伙伴在玩家格挡后承伤，超出伙伴生命的伤害仍由玩家承担。构筑可围绕多段指令、血量转伤害、牺牲、复活、灵魂喂养和召唤抽牌展开。
