## 英文描述（Workshop 正文，可直接粘贴）

**The Knight — a Hollow Knight character for Slay the Spire 2**

Play as the silent vessel from Hallownest. Strike enemies with your Nail to
gather SOUL, then unleash it in devastating spells.

**The SOUL loop:** Nail attacks gather 11 SOUL per hit (max 99). Spells cost
33 SOUL on top of their energy. Nail attacks never return SOUL from spells —
choose each turn: bank it, spend it.

**36 cards (+ the Weaver's Bite token) across six build directions:**
- **Multi-hit Nail arts** — Quick Slash, Channelled Nail, Coiled Nail,
  Dash Slash: every extra strike gathers more SOUL (and more nail strength)
- **Soul banking** — Kingsoul and the Void Heart relic: Nail damage scales
  with the SOUL you hold; hoard 66 and the Void pays it back as Energy
- **Shell & counters** — Baldur Shell, Stalwart Shell, Thorns of Agony,
  Steady Body, Lifeblood Cocoon: block, punish, and turn defense into SOUL
- **Spells** — Vengeful Spirit, Abyss Shriek, Desolate Dive, Deep Focus:
  amplified by Shaman Stone, healed by the Awakened Dream
- **Low-HP fury** — Fury of the Fallen and Stalwart Shell reward a cracked shell
- **Summons** — Weaversong spins a free Weaver's Bite into your hand every turn

**14 relics** including the starter Soul Catcher, the banking duo (Soul
Vessel, Void Heart), and tempo pieces (Sprintmaster's Gift, Hiveblood,
Lifeblood Core).

Every card face uses official game sprites or official art icons; the
Knight stands with you in combat (breathing glow and drifting SOUL wisps),
at rest sites, shops, and even in death.

**The Knight now speaks with every Ancient in the spire** — each has a
first-visit scene and a repeating one.

**Available in English and Simplified Chinese (简体中文)** — follows the
game's language setting automatically.

### v0.3.6 (2026-09-15) — 数值平衡轮 / Balance Pass

**中文**

- 对标原版角色卡牌数值基准，全面校准：
- 快速劈砍：8 点伤害 → **3 次 3 点伤害**（升级每段 +1，共 12）——快速连砍改为多段
- 蓄能骨钉：3×3 → **4×3**；大斩：16 → **18**
- 旋风斩：5 → **8** AOE；钉气冲击：技能牌 → **攻击牌**，7 → **8** AOE（气浪依然不产魂）
- 冲刺：6+6 → **7+7**；深渊尖啸：20 → **24**；暗影之魂：30 → **32**；梦之钉：3 → **6**

**English**

- Calibrated against vanilla card benchmarks (Strike 6/1, Twin Strike 5x2,
  Echoing Slash 10 AOE, Bludgeon 32/3):
- Quick Slash: 8 → **3x3 damage** (upgrade +1 per hit) — rapid slashes are
  multi-hit now
- Channelled Nail: 3x3 → **4x3**; Great Slash: 16 → **18**
- Cyclone Slash: 5 → **8** AOE; Nail Shockwave: Skill → **Attack**, 7 → **8**
  AOE (still gathers no SOUL)
- Dash: 6/6 → **7/7**; Abyss Shriek: 20 → **24**; Shade Soul: 30 → **32**;
  Dream Nail: 3 → **6**
