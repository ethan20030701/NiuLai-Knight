# v0.3.3 更新公告 / Update Notes

## 中文

**《空洞骑士》角色 Mod v0.3.3 — 机制修复 + 界面焕新**

### 机制修复（重要）
- **修复血胆之茧、巴德尔之壳、影披风三张牌的效果从未真正生效的问题**：这三个"持续本回合"的增益此前会在你自己的回合结束时提前消失——而它们的正题（格挡吸收得魂、被击反伤、抵挡下一次敌人攻击）全都发生在敌人回合。现在它们会正确持续到你的下一个回合开始（与原版荆棘同款时序）
- 修复全部能力（Power）悬停提示显示"获得 0 点"的问题，现在显示实际数值
- 补上血胆之茧、影披风缺失的能力图标
- 修复失败结算画面中小骑士不播放死亡动画的问题

### 界面焕新
- 灵魂（SOUL）图标重新设计：白色面具小圆球（就是小骑士本人的样子），战斗计数器与卡牌描述内联图标统一
- 灵魂计数器移到血条上方，不再与能量球和增益/减益图标重叠
- 战斗中的小骑士改为面朝敌人的姿态；商店里站上垫子、面朝商人
- 地图路径与小骑士名字颜色改为苍白灵魂蓝

> **需要前置 BaseLib (v3.4.7+)**。欢迎在评论区反馈数值与体验！

## English

**The Knight — Hollow Knight Character Mod v0.3.3 — Mechanics Fixes + UI Refresh**

### Mechanics fixes (important)
- **Fixed Lifeblood Cocoon, Baldur Shell and Shade Cloak never actually working**: these "this turn" buffs used to expire at the end of YOUR turn — but everything they do (block-absorb SOUL, riposte damage, dodging the next enemy attack) happens during the ENEMY turn. They now last until your next turn starts (same timing as vanilla Reflect)
- Fixed all Power hover tips showing "gain 0" instead of the real value
- Added the missing Lifeblood Cocoon / Shade Cloak power icons
- Fixed the Knight not playing his death animation on the game-over screen

### UI refresh
- Redesigned the SOUL icon: a little pale mask orb (the Knight himself), shared by the combat counter and inline card text
- Moved the SOUL counter above the HP bar — no more overlapping the energy orb or buff icons
- The Knight now faces the enemies in combat, and faces the merchant while standing on the mat in shops
- Map path and name color changed to pale soul blue

> **Requires BaseLib (v3.4.7+)**. Feedback on balance is welcome!
