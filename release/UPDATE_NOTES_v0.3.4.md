# v0.3.4 更新公告 / Update Notes

## 中文

**《空洞骑士》角色 Mod v0.3.4 — 剑技归位 + 战斗反馈**

### 机制
- **钉气冲击现在正确吃到骨钉加成**：它是骨钉打出的剑技，此前因为"技能牌"的身份吃不到脆弱之力 / 不碎之力 / 磨刀石 / 堕落之怒 / 剑技遗物的加成，现在全部生效（依然不产魂——灵魂只来自真正的骨钉命中）
- **灵魂图腾重做**：旧版 2 费 + 33 灵魂换 2 能量，净收益为零，形同虚设。现在 **1 费 + 33 灵魂 → 获得 3 点能量并抽 1 张牌**（升级后抽 2 张），终于是一笔值得做的交易
- **战斗反馈**：小骑士被攻击时会受击抖动 + 闪烁，攻击命中时会向敌人突进——不再是站桩木桩

### 视觉
- **九张剑技 / 法术卡面全部换用原作官方图标**：大斩、冲刺斩、旋风斩、暗影之魂、嚎叫幽灵、深渊尖啸、荒芜俯冲、黑暗降临、影披风——深渊渐变背景 + 官方图标，一眼原作
- 小骑士体型缩小（战斗与商店），终于比铁甲战士矮了——他可是"小"骑士
- 卡牌描述里的灵魂小图标尺寸修正

### 修复
- 修复能力悬停提示显示"获得 0 点"的问题（上版遗漏说明：所有 Power 数值提示均正确显示）
- 补上血胆之茧、影披风缺失的能力图标

> **需要前置 BaseLib (v3.4.7+)**。欢迎反馈！

## English

**The Knight — Hollow Knight Character Mod v0.3.4 — Nail Arts Take Effect + Combat Feedback**

### Mechanics
- **Nail Shockwave now benefits from nail strength**: it is a nail technique, but its Skill type excluded it from Fragile Strength / Unbreakable Strength / Whetstone / Fury of the Fallen / Nailmaster's Glory. All apply now (still gathers no SOUL — that stays exclusive to real nail hits)
- **Soul Totem redesigned**: the old version traded 2 energy + 33 SOUL for 2 energy — a net of nothing. Now **1 energy + 33 SOUL → gain 3 Energy and draw 1 card** (upgraded: draw 2), a deal actually worth taking
- **Combat feedback**: the Knight shakes and flickers when hit, and lunges at the enemies when his attacks connect — no more standing like a mannequin

### Visuals
- **All nine nail-art / spell card faces now use the official game icons**: Great Slash, Dash Slash, Cyclone Slash, Shade Soul, Howling Wraiths, Abyss Shriek, Desolate Dive, Descending Dark, Shade Cloak — abyss gradient + official icon
- The Knight is smaller in combat and at shops, as befits the LITTLE knight
- Fixed the inline SOUL glyph size in card text

> **Requires BaseLib (v3.4.7+)**. Feedback welcome!
