The Knight — a Hollow Knight character for Slay the Spire 2

Play as the silent vessel from Hallownest. Strike enemies with your Nail to gather SOUL, then unleash it in devastating spells.

The SOUL loop: Nail attacks gather 11 SOUL per hit (max 99). Spells cost 33 SOUL on top of their energy. Nail attacks never return SOUL from spells — choose each turn: bank it, spend it.

86 cards (+ the Weaver's Bite token) across build directions — v0.5.0 + v0.6.0 added a full Poison line, new card engines, and filled every rarity tier to parity with the vanilla cast:

- Poison (new) — Spore Breath seeds it, Venom Blade loads a heavy dose, Defender's Crest seeps it onto ALL enemies every turn, Envenomed Nail carries it on every nail strike, and Catalyst multiplies it ×2/×3: a full damage-over-time line

- Companions & echoes — Dreamshield guards your next three turn-starts; Grimmchild spits embers at a random enemy every turn-end; After Image gains Block for every card played; Raging Nail copies itself into the discard pile

- Spell school — Spellcharge, Soulbell, Full Soul, Soul Torrent, Vengeful Spirit, Abyss Shriek, Shade Soul, Howling Wraiths, Descending Dark, Hallownest's Echo (a flat 26-damage wave) and the Soul Tyrant kill-loop

- Nail school — Hum, Quick Slash, Nail Flurry, Nail Storm (X-cost, hits EVERY enemy, gathers SOUL per hit), Broken Vessel, Ricochet Nail, Twin Slash, alongside Charged Arts, Pride's Mark and the Sword Arts

- Soul banking — Kingsoul, Full Soul, Soul Vessel, Void Heart, Soul Unbound, Soul Aegis (Block equal to your SOUL), Soul Battery and Salubra's Blessing: hold your SOUL, get paid for it

- Shell & defense — Impregnable (Block no longer expires), Shell Slam (damage = your Block), Second Wind (exhaust your hand into Block), Void Feast (kills permanently grow your Max HP), Carefree Melody (survive death itself)

- Injured & Focus — Grubsong's Melody, Lifeblood Cocoon, Fury of the Fallen, Pain Brand (wounds become Strength), Focus, Deep Focus, Shape of Unn

23 exclusive relics — from the starter Soul Catcher and the banking duo (Soul Vessel, Void Heart) to Gathering Swarm (+25% gold), Quick Focus (Focus costs 22 SOUL), Soul Candle, Void Brand and Hallownest Seal — plus every shared relic the spire offers.

All 88 card portraits now use illustrated Hallownest scenes. The character selection cover and starter Soul Catcher relic have new art. In combat, the Knight draws the Nail from his back for a six-frame swing, then returns it after the attack; SOUL spells, skills and Powers have their own effects. He also appears at rest sites and shops, and every Ancient has something to say to him.

Available in English and Simplified Chinese — follows the game's language setting automatically.

Requires BaseLib (v3.4.7 or newer).
