The Knight — a Hollow Knight character for Slay the Spire 2

Play as the silent vessel from Hallownest. Strike enemies with your Nail to gather SOUL, then unleash it in devastating spells.

The SOUL loop: Nail attacks gather 11 SOUL per hit (max 99). Spells cost 33 SOUL on top of their energy. Nail attacks never return SOUL from spells — choose each turn: bank it, spend it.

54 cards (+ the Weaver's Bite token) across build directions — v0.4.0 brings three new companions into the loop:

- Companions & echoes (new) — Dreamshield spins a woven shield that guards your next three turn-starts; Soul Surge gives the spell line a cheap common workhorse (damage and a card); Grimmchild spits embers at a random enemy every turn-end

- Spell school — Spellcharge (every Skill charges your next Spell), Soulbell (your first Spell each turn draws), Full Soul (a brimming vessel empowers Spells), Soul Torrent (pour X energy and 33 SOUL into one torrent), alongside Vengeful Spirit, Abyss Shriek, Shade Soul, Howling Wraiths, Descending Dark and the Soul Tyrant kill-loop

- Nail school — Hum (a free strike), Quick Slash (0-cost), Nail Flurry (X hits, every one gathers SOUL), Broken Vessel (wound yourself, strike harder), alongside Charged Arts, Pride's Mark and the Sword Arts

- Soul banking — Kingsoul, Full Soul, Soul Vessel, Void Heart and Soul Unbound: hold your SOUL to empower strikes, then unleash everything in one eruption

- Consume echo — Broken Shell and the Grubsong Eater relic: every exhausted card hardens the shell and gathers SOUL (Weaver's Bites feed the wall)

- Injured — Grubsong's Melody, Lifeblood Cocoon, Carefree Melody: wounds become SOUL, and death itself gets a second chance

- Focus — Focus, Deep Focus, Shape of Unn: the vessel's way to mend

15 relics including the starter Soul Catcher, the banking duo (Soul Vessel, Void Heart), and tempo pieces (Sprintmaster's Gift, Hiveblood, Lifeblood Core).

Every card face uses official game sprites or official art icons; the Knight stands with you in combat, at rest sites, shops — and every Ancient in the spire has something to say to the Knight.

Available in English and Simplified Chinese — follows the game's language setting automatically.

Requires BaseLib (v3.4.7 or newer).
