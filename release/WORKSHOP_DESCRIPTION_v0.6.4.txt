The Knight — Hollow Knight Character for Slay the Spire 2
Current version: v0.6.4

Play as the silent vessel from Hallownest. Strike enemies with your Nail to gather SOUL, then spend it on spells, healing and companions.

v0.6.4 fixes
- Fixes cards getting stuck in the play area on stable v0.107.1, including Old Nail.
- Supports the combat API differences between stable v0.107.1 and public-beta v0.111.0.
- Fixes death prevention, one-hit dodging, multi-hit charge bonuses, Hatchling death bursts, ownership checks and Abyss Shriek scaling.
- Corrects several card and relic descriptions.

Content
- 104 card types: 101 reward-pool cards, 2 basic cards and the Weaver's Bite token.
- 25 exclusive relics, including the starter Soul Catcher.
- English and Simplified Chinese, following the game's language setting.
- Requires BaseLib v3.4.7 or newer. Please subscribe to the required BaseLib item.

The SOUL loop
- Nail hits gather 11 SOUL with the starter relic; the base SOUL cap is 99.
- Most spells cost 33 SOUL in addition to Energy. Spells and companion attacks do not gather Nail SOUL.
- Build around Nail combos, spell bursts, SOUL banking, Poison, shell defense, injury rewards, or the new companion archetype.

New in v0.6.3: companion summoning
- 15 new cards and 2 new relics support three companions with independent HP bars.
- Weaverling attacks at turn end and specializes in multi-hit commands.
- Grimmchild grows through repeated summons: its flame deals 5, then 8, then 11 damage, with the original stage artwork changing as it matures.
- A Glowing Womb Hatchling guards first and explodes when it dies or is sacrificed.
- One living companion of each species. Summoning a living species increases both its current and maximum HP.
- Companions intercept enemy attacks after your Block. Damage exceeding the guarding companion's HP still hits you.
- Grow a companion for a health-based attack, chain commands into a four-hit finisher, sacrifice it for Block or resources, and revive the last fallen species.
- Spend SOUL to feed your companions, protect them with healing, and draw cards from summoning synergies.
- Companion attacks do not inherit Nail strength or extra Nail hits.
- Companion sprites, charm icons and the new card scenes use original Hollow Knight assets collected from the Hollow Knight Wiki. Soul Unbound's missing portrait is also restored.

Presentation
Hallownest card scenes, character selection artwork, Nail draw-and-slash animation, SOUL casting effects, rest-site and shop appearances, and character-specific Ancient dialogue.

v0.6.3 虫群召唤流
- 新增 15 张卡牌、2 件辅助遗物，以及各有独立血条的织者幼虫、格林之子和孵化小虫。
- 同种伙伴最多存活一只；重复召唤增加当前和最大生命。格林之子最多成长两次，火焰伤害 5→8→11，并切换原版立绘。
- 玩家格挡后，伙伴承受敌方攻击；单次攻击超过伙伴生命的伤害仍由玩家承担。孵化小虫优先挡伤，死亡或被牺牲时爆炸。
- 构筑可围绕血量转伤害、多段指令与追击、牺牲换格挡或资源、复活、灵魂喂养和召唤抽牌展开。
- 新遗物「编织者之歌」让每次召唤额外增加 3 生命；「蜂群共鸣」在回合开始有至少两只伙伴存活时抽 1 张牌。
- 召唤物与护符改用 Wiki 收录的《空洞骑士》原版素材，修正小织者形象，补齐「魂裂」卡面。
