# v0.3.5 更新公告 / Update Notes

## 中文

**《空洞骑士》小骑士角色 Mod v0.3.5 — 官方贴图卡面 + 三件新遗物 + 关键修复**

### 重大修复
- **修复第二层古代事件（达弗等）可能永久卡死的问题**：此前如果存档里其他角色已经见过达弗，小骑士首次到达弗处会没有任何对话和选项，事件无法进行。现在补齐了小骑士与全部 8 位原版古代（达弗/尼奥/诺努佩佩/坦克/瓦库/奥罗巴斯/帕尔/特兹卡塔拉）的专属对话——每位都有初见剧情与回访对话。
- 修复深凝、织者之咬描述中「消耗」字样重复显示的问题（关键词横幅自动渲染，文本不再手写）。

### 卡面全面升级（12 张）
以下卡面从程序绘制换用**游戏原版官方贴图**：旧骨钉、蓄能骨钉、盘绕骨钉、灵魂图腾、血胆之茧、巴德尔之壳、蛞蝓之形、磨刀石、稳固之壳、织者之歌、萨满石、国魂——原作像素级质感，与已发布的官方图标卡面风格统一。

### 新增 3 件遗物
- **冲刺大师**（罕见）：你每回合打出的第一张技能牌，抽 1 张牌。
- **蜂巢之血**（罕见）：每当你失去生命值，本回合下一张法术牌能量消耗减少 1 点。
- **生命血之心**（普通）：战斗开始时获得 8 点格挡；每当你失去生命值，获得 3 点灵魂。

### 其他
- 嚎叫幽灵 / 暗影之魂 / 黑暗降临的描述现在明确写出「消耗 33 灵魂」。
- 联机安全审计：织者之咬、灵魂图腾、虚空之心在合作模式下的归属判定与原版一致。

> **需要前置 BaseLib (v3.4.7+)**。欢迎反馈！

## English

**The Knight — Hollow Knight Character Mod v0.3.5 — official sprite card faces + 3 new relics + critical fix**

### Critical fix
- **Fixed a potential permanent freeze at Ancient events (Darv etc.) in Act 2+**: if another character had already met Darv in your save, the Knight's first visit showed no dialogue and no options, soft-locking the run. The Knight now has dedicated dialogue with **all 8 vanilla Ancients** (Darv, Neow, Nonupeipe, Tanx, Vakuu, Orobas, Pael, Tezcatara) — each with a first-visit scene and a repeating one.
- Fixed the word "Exhaust" appearing twice on Deep Focus and Weaver's Bite (the keyword banner already renders it).

### Card faces rebuilt (12)
These faces now use **official game sprites** instead of procedural art: Old Nail, Channelled Nail, Coiled Nail, Soul Totem, Lifeblood Cocoon, Baldur Shell, Shape of Unn, Whetstone, Stalwart Shell, Weaversong, Shaman Stone, Kingsoul.

### 3 new relics
- **Sprintmaster's Gift** (Rare): The first Skill you play each turn draws 1 card.
- **Hiveblood** (Rare): Whenever you lose HP, the next Spell you play this turn costs 1 less Energy.
- **Lifeblood Core** (Common): Gain 8 Block at the start of each combat; whenever you lose HP, gain 3 SOUL.

### Misc
- Howling Wraiths / Shade Soul / Descending Dark descriptions now state the 33 SOUL cost explicitly.
- Co-op audit: Weaver's Bite, Soul Totem and Void Heart ownership gating matches vanilla behavior.

> **Requires BaseLib (v3.4.7+)**. Feedback welcome!
